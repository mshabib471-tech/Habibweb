Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our product, pricing, features, and support — all in one place.

What kind of devices do you repair?

We repair all major electronics including smartphones (Apple, Android), laptops, desktops, tablets, and smartwatches. Our team is equipped to handle both hardware replacements and software diagnostics.

Do you use original replacement parts?

Yes, we strictly source 100% genuine and high-grade OEM parts directly from authorized suppliers to ensure your device functions perfectly and lasts longer.

How long does a typical repair take?

Most standard repairs (like screen or battery replacements) are completed within 1-2 hours on the same day. Complex motherboard issues may take 2-3 business days.
