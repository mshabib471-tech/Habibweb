Our Story

From a Small Desk to
Your Trusted Repair Hub

Habib iFix started with a simple belief — that broken devices don't need to be thrown away. What began as a small workspace with basic tools has grown into a full-service repair center trusted by hundreds of customers across Chattogram.

Our founder, Habibur Rahman, spent years mastering the art of electronics repair — from smartphone micro-soldering to laptop motherboard-level fixes. His dedication to quality and transparency became the foundation of everything we do.

Today, we handle everything from cracked iPhone screens to complex water damage recovery, always using genuine parts and providing honest diagnostics. Every device that enters our workshop leaves better than before.

Meet The Founder

Habibur Rahman

Founder & Lead Technician

With over 5 years of hands-on experience in electronics repair, Habibur Rahman founded Habib iFix with a mission to bring world-class repair services to Chattogram at affordable prices.

His expertise spans across smartphone micro-soldering, laptop motherboard repair, data recovery, and camera servicing. He personally oversees every complex repair to ensure the highest quality standards.

When he's not fixing devices, Habibur creates educational content about tech repair on YouTube, sharing his knowledge with aspiring technicians across Bangladesh.
